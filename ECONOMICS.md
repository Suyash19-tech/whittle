# ECONOMICS.md (Unit Economics & Business Logic)

## Overview: Whittle as a Lead-Gen Engine
Whittle isn't built to be a standalone $20/mo SaaS tool. That would be a distraction. Instead, Whittle is designed as a **high-intent lead generation layer** for Credex's broader procurement and credit offerings.

The goal of every audit isn't a subscription fee; it’s identifying an **AI-heavy startup** and routing them into a high-trust sales conversation.

---

# 1. The Value of a "Converted" Lead
Our target customers are seed to Series A startups (5–50 employees) who are currently spending $1,000–$15,000/month on AI APIs and SaaS tools (OpenAI, Anthropic, Cursor, Pinecone, etc.).

When Whittle identifies these companies, the potential Lifetime Value (LTV) for Credex is significant:

| Metric | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Average Customer ARR (Procurement/Credit) | $6,000 |
| Gross Margin Contribution | ~70% |
| Estimated Retention | 2–3 Years |
| **Estimated LTV** | **$12,000 – $18,000** |

Even with a conservative capture rate, every audit that identifies >$500/mo in waste is a potential five-figure relationship for Credex.

---

# 2. Driving Down CAC via "Engineering Trust"
We’ve intentionally built Whittle to avoid the high Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) of generic SaaS marketing. By focusing on **technical communities**, we keep acquisition costs low.

- **Organic "Stack Roasts" (X/Reddit)**: Cost is effectively $0. The "Shareable Report" loops we built generate visibility every time a founder posts their waste score.
- **Manual Founder Outreach**: High-touch but low-cost ($50–$150 CAC). Because the engine produces **deterministic, explainable math**, technical founders trust the output more than a generic marketing tool.

---

# 3. The Conversion Funnel
Based on the current MVP's UX flow, here is the predicted funnel:

| Funnel Stage | Conservative Target |
|---|---|
| **Audit Completed** | 1,000 / month |
| **Consultation Booked** (via our savings-threshold CTA) | 10% (100) |
| **Qualified Sales Conversation** | 50% (50) |
| **Closed Procurement/Credit Account** | 20% (10) |

**Result**: 10 new customers/month × $6k ARR = **$60,000 Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) Pipeline.**

---

# 4. Scaling to $1M ARR in 18 Months
To hit $1M ARR, Credex needs approximately **167 active paying customers**. 

The math to get there:
- We need ~10 new paying customers per month.
- This requires ~1,000 completed audits per month.

This scale is achievable because of the **"Shareable Intelligence"** we built into the product. When one founder shares a report saying they saved $400/month, the social proof does the selling for us.

---

# 5. The Critical Engineering Factor
The biggest risk in this economic model is the **"Trust Decay."** If the audit engine produces fake or exaggerated savings, the funnel collapses immediately.

That is why we prioritized:
- **Deterministic Scoring**: No "AI magic" for the math—just real rules.
- **KEEP Verdicts**: Validating what's *working* is just as important as flagging waste. It builds the credibility needed to book that consultation call.
- **Centralized Pricing**: Ensuring every calculation matches the **PRICING_DATA.md** single source of truth.

**Whittle is a precision instrument for lead generation, designed to convert tooling fatigue into long-term financial relationships.**
