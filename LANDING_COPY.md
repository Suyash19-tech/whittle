# FAQ

## 1. Does Whittle connect directly to our billing systems?

No. The current MVP uses manually entered stack information. This was intentional to keep onboarding friction low and make audits fast to complete.

---

## 2. How does Whittle decide which tools overlap?

The audit engine uses deterministic capability categories and stack-level analysis to detect likely overlap between:
- AI assistants
- coding tools
- research tools
- creative tools

Recommendations are explainable rather than AI-generated guesses.

---

## 3. Will the tool always recommend cutting costs?

No. Healthy stacks can receive KEEP verdicts if the tooling appears well-aligned with the team’s workflow. The goal is optimization clarity, not forced cost reduction.

---

## 4. Is this designed for enterprises?

The current MVP is optimized primarily for:
- startup teams
- engineering-heavy companies
- indie builders
- AI-native workflows

especially teams between 1–50 people.

---

## 5. Why not just use ChatGPT to analyze subscriptions manually?

General-purpose LLMs can generate suggestions, but they often produce inconsistent or unrealistic procurement advice. Whittle uses deterministic pricing and overlap logic so recommendations remain explainable, repeatable, and financially grounded.
